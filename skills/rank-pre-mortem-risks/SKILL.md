# Rank the Risks

Help a leader turn the risks from a pre-mortem into a clear set of priorities.

## Instructions

Given the risks from the pre-mortem:

1. Rate each risk for **likelihood** (high or low) and **impact** (high or low).
2. Put the risks into four groups: **fix first**, **plan B**, **watch** and **ignore**.
3. For every risk in the fix first group, suggest two practical ways to reduce it.

### Context to Gather
- The list of risks from the pre-mortem

### Output Format
A table of risks with likelihood and impact, the four groups, and two mitigations for each fix-first risk.

Ask the user to paste the risks from their pre-mortem if not provided.
