# Run a Pre-mortem

Help a leader warm up their own thinking before a pre-mortem session with their team.

## Instructions

Imagine it is six months from now and the project has failed.

1. Give 12 reasons it failed.
2. Cover missed deadlines, quality problems, people pulling in different directions, customer response and things outside the team's control.
3. Be blunt.
4. Group the 12 reasons into themes.

### Context to Gather
- What the project is
- Who is involved
- The deadline
- What is at stake

### Output Format
A list of 12 failure reasons, then the same reasons grouped under theme headings.

### Considerations
- To prepare for leading the session use prepare-to-listen
- After the team has shared their risks, use rank-pre-mortem-risks

Ask the user for the project and its context if not provided.
