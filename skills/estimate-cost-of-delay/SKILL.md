# Estimate the Cost of Delay

Help a product leader put a number on what one week of delay costs their team.

## Instructions

Given what the user knows about their team and product:

1. Build a rough calculation of the cost of one week of delay.
2. List every assumption the calculation depends on.
3. Mark each assumption as **high**, **medium** or **low** confidence.
4. State the final figure (or range) clearly at the end.

### Context to Gather
- Team size
- The product
- Revenue or usage figures
- How long the problem currently takes to solve

### Output Format
A short step-by-step calculation, a table or list of assumptions with confidence ratings, and the resulting figure.

### Considerations
- Use plain words, no jargon. The figure has to make sense to a leadership team.
- Prefer a range over false precision when assumptions are low confidence

Ask the user for team size, product, revenue or usage, and how long the problem takes to solve today if not provided.
