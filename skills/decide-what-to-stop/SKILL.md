# What Should We Stop Doing?

Help a product leader decide what their team should stop doing to free up time.

## Instructions

Given a list of what the team does in a normal week:

1. Pick the five things you would retire first.
2. For each, explain why it is a good candidate to stop.
3. For each, describe the risk of stopping it.
4. Rank the five by how much time they would free up.

### Context to Gather
- The team's rituals, meetings, reports and tools in a normal week

### Output Format
A ranked list of five items, each with the reason to retire it, the risk of stopping it and a rough sense of the time it frees up.

Ask the user for the list of rituals, meetings, reports and tools if not provided.
