# Prepare to Listen

Help a leader prepare to listen well when they run a pre-mortem with their team.

## Instructions

Given:
- **The team** the pre-mortem is with
- **The leader's habits**: For example talking too much, defending the plan or moving too fast

1. Give five things the leader can say to make the room feel safe.
2. Give three habits the leader should avoid during the session, tailored to the habits they named.

### Context to Gather
- Which team the pre-mortem is with
- What the leader tends to do that could shut people down

### Output Format
Two short lists, five things to say (as exact phrases) and three habits to avoid.

### Considerations
- To run the pre-mortem itself use run-pre-mortem

Ask the user which team the pre-mortem is with and what habits they tend to fall into if not provided.
